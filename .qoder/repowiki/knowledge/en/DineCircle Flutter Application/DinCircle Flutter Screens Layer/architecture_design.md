Each subdirectory under lib/screens/ is a feature-scoped screen (or small screen cluster) that owns its own route entry but delegates state to Riverpod providers defined outside this layer; screens are pure presentational consumers of those providers. Cross-cutting concerns — theming, shared widgets, navigation helpers, and provider wiring — live in sibling packages (lib/providers, lib/widgets, lib/navigation), so screens stay thin. The current work focuses on the booking_flow child (bookings_screen.dart at ~2450 lines), which is being audited for overflow fixes, compact card layouts, status-gated actions, and a corrected directions bottom sheet before production release.
